Build a private refinancing application on Canton. A borrower replaces an existing lender using the incoming lender's funds. One committed ledger update pays the outgoing lender, closes its loan, transfers collateral control and activates the replacement loan. Users: borrower, outgoing lender A and incoming lender B.
Use this fixed test example: A's payoff is 101,000 test dollars; B lends 100,000; the borrower contributes 1,000; 150 test collateral units move from securing A's loan to securing B's loan. The new debt is 100,000. The borrower avoids finding the entire payoff upfront; this is not a reduction in debt by 100,000.

Assign contract/ledger work and frontend/backend work. The roughly three-week estimate assumes two full-time developers, one already productive in Daml.

Privacy
Query active contracts and transaction history as each lender: A cannot see B's private rate/maturity; B cannot see A's private historical terms.
Failed closing
Insufficient funds, expired/changed approvals, replay and direct collateral-release bypass attempts cannot produce a partial or unsecured result.
Cleanup
Unused preparation allocations can be withdrawn or cancelled. Failed closing preserves pre-closing state; it does not undo previously committed preparation.
